hanji 한지

Thin, strong, and it takes whatever ink you bring.

한지 is Korean mulberry paper — the surface things are drawn on. This system is that surface for apps: one token contract, a suite of components and a motion vocabulary, extracted from a shipped music player. Every rule in it was a bug on a real phone first.

  1. i

    Performance is the first feature

    Animation runs on the UI thread. Transforms, never layout. One clock per surface, and nothing ticks when it is idle.

  2. ii

    Themable, not themed

    Components read one semantic contract and nothing else. Change the paper, the ink or the density and every screen repaints.

  3. iii

    hanji owns reduced motion

    Loops never arm, entrances become fades, travel becomes stillness. Feedback survives; decoration goes first.

  4. iv

    Adapter over dependency

    Icons, haptics and storage are interfaces the app implements. No icon set ships in the library.

  5. v

    Accessibility is the floor

    Sliders answer swipes, composite rows are one focus stop, toasts are announced, and every target is 44 points.

Foundations

01

A component names a token; it never names a value. Three papers, twelve inks and one set of springs are the whole contract an app has to satisfy. Depth comes from paper steps and hairlines, not shadows — they cost nothing to draw and survive both a white page and a true-black one.

    02

    hanji moves like a sheet of paper on a table: it answers the finger at once, travels a short way, and settles without fuss. Motion explains what changed — it is never the show.       §1Animate transforms, never layout

      Width, height and top are skipped on gesture-driven frames in release builds: the thumb moves and the fill freezes. Draw full size inside a clip and move it.
