Surfaces

05

Surfaces float over the page: sheets, dialogs, toasts, menus. hanji has no shadow scale, so a surface separates with a black scrim, one surface step and a hairline. The one declared exception is a soft lift under anchored popovers, which have no scrim to stand on. Every surface grows from where it came from and leaves faster than it arrived.

    05.01

    Sheet

    Options or detail that rise from the bottom edge and keep the screen behind in view. It rests at medium or large and follows the finger between them. Not for a decision that blocks the task; that is a dialog.

    Shape
    inset 8 · radius lg · surface-2 + hairline
    Detents
    medium 50% · large 90% (max)
    Drag
    projects velocity × .15 s · dismiss past 25% · overdrag ÷ 3
    Scrim
    black · .55 × how far it is open
    Spring
    open gentle · release snappy, keeps velocity · close snappy clamped
    Mount
    rows mount after it lands; a quiet line holds the room (§12)
    A11y
    dialog · handle is a button (resize) · Esc closes

    The close is clamped because unmounting waits on it; a spring is visually done long before it is mathematically done.

    05.02

    Dialog

    A centred question that stops the task until it is answered. Say what happens to what, then offer at most two actions. Destructive confirms put focus on the safe choice.

    Width
    ≤ 380 · radius xl · surface-2 + hairline
    Actions
    ≤ 2 · destructive = danger tint, never a solid red
    Enter
    scale .94 → 1 + fade · snappy, clamped
    Exit
    fade + scale .98 · fast (180)
    Scrim
    black · .55 · tap outside = cancel
    Focus
    lands on Cancel · trapped · Esc · returns to the opener

    Scale is multiplied by --travel: under reduced motion the dialog only fades.

    05.03

    Toast

    A short line that confirms something already happened, then leaves on its own. It can carry one action, usually Undo. Never for anything the user must read or decide; that is a banner or a dialog.

    Life
    3200 ms · +1800 with an action · min 1200
    Queue
    cap 3 · older ones step back ×.95, lift 10
    Enter
    rise 18 + fade · gentle · the stack re-seats on gentle
    Exit
    fade + drop 8 · fast · swipe past 70 or flick
    Paint
    surface-2 + hairline · glyph by tone (success · danger)
    A11y
    status (alert for danger) · paused while held or focused
    API
    H.toast(msg, {tone, action, host})

    When a fourth arrives the oldest leaves first, so the stack never grows taller than three.

    Tones · tap one to send it

    Hold a toast to pause it. Swipe sideways, or tap it, to dismiss.

    05.04

    Menu

    A short list of actions anchored to the control that opened it. It grows out of that control and flips above it when there is no room below. Destructive actions sit last, after a rule.

    Placement
    gap 6 to the anchor · margin 8 to the edge · flips
    Shape
    radius md · surface-2 · hairline + lift (declared)
    Rows
    40 · icon 18 · rules group the verbs
    Enter
    scale .92 → 1 from the anchor's corner · snappy
    Exit
    fade + scale · fast
    A11y
    menu / menuitem · ↑ ↓ Home End · Esc returns focus

    The origin is the anchor's corner, so the menu reads as coming out of the ⋯ button, not appearing near it.

    Popover & tooltip New

    A tooltip names an icon-only control; it never holds anything you need to act on. A popover holds a small task in place, like renaming, so the user keeps their spot.

    Tooltip
    fg on bg (inverted) · 12/16 · radius xs · arrow 8
    Delay
    500 ms · then warm: instant for 400 ms after one hides
    Touch
    long-press 500 ms · hides 1.2 s after release
    Popover
    surface-2 · radius md · lift · arrow on the anchor
    Motion
    scale .95 + 4 from the arrow side · snappy in, fast out
    A11y
    aria-describedby · popover = dialog · Enter saves · Esc

    The warm period means moving along a toolbar reads every label without waiting again.

    05.07

    Banner

    A message that stays on the page until it is dealt with. Every tone is glyph + word + sentence, never colour alone, and offers the next step. Dismissible unless it blocks something.

    Anatomy
    glyph 20 · bold word · sentence · action · dismiss
    Tones
    neutral · success · warning · danger (tint + hairline)
    Shape
    radius md · tone at 14% fill, 26% hairline
    Dismiss
    fade + scale .98 (fast) → rows close up (gentle, clamped)
    A11y
    status · danger = alert · dismiss has a name

    Closing the gap is this chapter's one layout transition: a transform cannot hand the space back. It starts only after the fade, and nothing else moves while it runs.

    05.10

    Overlay screen

    A full screen that rises over the current one for a focused view, such as lyrics or a queue, and drops back to exactly where you were. Pull the header down to leave.

    Rise
    from the bottom edge · gentle · drag-down to close
    Exit
    snappy clamped · keeps the finger's velocity
    Content
    fades in over .35 → .88 of the rise, rising 28
    Chrome
    close 24 (fg-dim) · title + source
    Rule
    hosts its own GestureHandlerRootView (PRINCIPLES §7); without it every gesture inside is dead on Android
    A11y
    dialog · focus to close · Esc · page behind is inert

    Lyrics are content that mounts after the entrance; they hold back until the surface has nearly landed.
