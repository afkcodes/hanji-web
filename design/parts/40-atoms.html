Atoms

03

Atoms are the smallest parts with an opinion. hanji has three, and holds them everywhere: actions are pills, depth is a hairline rather than a shadow, and there is one accent — it means “act here” or “this one”, never decoration.

    03.02

    Icon

    hanji ships no icon set. The app implements one function, and every component asks it for a glyph by name, size and tone — so the library never fights your brand’s icons.

    Contract
    render({ name, size, weight, tone, color })
    Sizes
    16 inline · 20 rows · 24 tabs · 28
    Stroke
    1.5 · round caps and joins
    Tones
    fg · fgDim · fgMute · accent · onAccent · danger · onScrim
    Weight
    linear · bold for the active tab
    A11y
    decorative; label the control, not the glyph

    Pressable

    The base of every control: it answers the finger before the finger lifts. Controls dip; rows dim — a row that shrank would nudge a whole list.

    Haptics

    03.04

    Squircle

    hanji’s corners are continuous, not circular: the curve starts earlier and eases into the straight, the way a machined edge does. At small radii you feel it more than see it — so here it is drawn large.

    Smoothing
    .72 surfaces · .6 small corners
    Radii
    xs 8 · sm 12 · md 16 · lg 20 · xl 28 · pill
    Native
    react-native-fast-squircle
    Web
    corner-shape: superellipse() where supported
    Rule
    one radius per element class

    Shape never animates between radii; a pressed surface keeps its corners.

    continuous · .72circular

    The circular corner leaves the straight at r; the continuous one starts bending at p = 1.72 r and eases in, so it never shows the kink where an arc meets a line. Hover a token to see its circular twin.

    03.06

    Spinner New

    An ink ring for work that takes a moment. It waits 400 ms before it appears, so fast work never flashes a spinner at all — and once shown it stays long enough to be read.

    Ring
    2 px ink tail to a round head (3 px at 40)
    Sizes
    16 inline · 20 buttons · 40 page
    Cadence
    one turn / 900 ms · linear, never eased
    Delay
    appears after 400 ms · stays ≥ 600 ms
    Reduced
    no rotation — opacity .55 ↔ .9
    A11y
    role=progressbar · aria-busy on the region

    A constant turn with a fading tail. Any change of speed in a spinner reads as a stall, so this is the one loop in hanji with no easing.

    Skeleton

    A still block in the shape of what is coming, with one soft highlight passing over it. One calm signal — hanji tried a breathing pulse first and retired it on a real phone.

    Block

    Sweep
    fg ≤ 9%, band 70% wide, translated across
    Cadence
    1000 ms pass · 450 ms rest · one clock
    Swap
    blocks leave fast; content cascades in, 24 ms apart
    Reduced
    still block; the sweep never mounts
    Why
    the breath vanished on dark papers; breath + sweep read “weirdly alive” (device, 2026-08-21)

    The 450 ms rest is the point: a relentless sweep reads as urgency.

    03.12

    Scrim

    Text over imagery sits on a scrim: black, faded by opacity alone. A light scrim on a paper theme would hide nothing, so the scrim is black on every theme and the ink over it is always light.

    Colour
    black on every theme
    Fade
    one RGB · .72 → 0 over the lower 55%
    Ink
    on-scrim — never fg-mute over art
    Art shadow
    0 12 24 · .45 — imagery’s one drop
    Rule
    helpers build every fade; components never hand-roll stops (§23)

    Scrims fade with their content and never on their own; a scrim that arrives late flashes the art.
