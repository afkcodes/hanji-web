Data & lists

07

Lists are where an app spends its frames. In hanji every row is memoized and bound a viewport ahead, content mounts only after its surface has landed, and everything that moves on a list runs off one clock. A thousand rows should cost what one row costs.

    07.01

    List row

    One thing per row, read left to right: what it is, what it says, what you can do. A row is one focus stop and one tap target — never a card per item.

    Height
    56 min · padding 0 16
    Leading
    40 · art r-xs · icon in tint · avatar
    Trailing
    value (mono, right) · chevron · toggle · check
    Hairline
    inset to the text: 16 + 40 + 12
    Press
    opacity .75 · no scale
    States
    default · selected · disabled (.4)
    A11y
    one stop per row · radio rows: arrows

    Rows never shrink under the finger — a column of dipping rows reads as a wobble. The selected check is the only thing that springs.

    07.02

    Progress bar

    How far, and how much is left — in words beside the bar, never the bar alone. If you can't say how far, say what's happening and use the indeterminate sweep.

    Track
    4 · pill · tone at .22
    Fill
    full-width, translated inside the clip
    Spring
    snappy · clamped
    Unknown
    span 1/3 · sweep 1.4 s
    Tones
    accent · fg · danger
    Reduced
    sweep parks mid-track
    A11y
    role=progressbar · valuenow

    The fill is drawn at full width and slid, never resized — a width animation misses frames in release builds (PRINCIPLES §1), and the rounded cap keeps its shape.

    Progress ring

    The bar's compact twin, for a tile, a row's trailing slot or a button that is busy. The 96 summary splits one total into at most three parts, labelled with amounts.

    Summary

    Arc

    Unknown
    25% arc · one turn per 0.9 s
    Value

    spin stops · arc stays

    A ring reports a reading, so it eases rather than springs: a reading that overshoots says 104%.

    Marquee

    For the one line that must stay on one line — a title in a player bar. It moves only when the text truly overflows, and only while it is on screen.

    Dwell
    1200 ms at rest before each pass
    Speed
    45 px/s · linear
    Gap
    40 between the text and its repeat
    Slack
    2 px — a near-fit stays still
    Edges
    trailing fade at rest · both while moving
    Clock
    one frame clock · paused off-screen
    Reduced
    still, with an ellipsis

    Measure first: hanji's marquee once looped a 90-second pass because its measuring ghost stretched to a 4000 px box (PRINCIPLES §19). The readout below is that measurement.

    07.08

    Drag list

    Reorder by picking a row up. The grip lifts at once; anywhere else on the row, a long press does, so a scroll is never mistaken for a drag. Every drag has a keyboard twin.

    Lift
    long press 220 ms · grip: at once
    Held
    ×1.03 · surface-2 · the one shadow
    Others
    slide one row · snappy · clamped
    Drop
    gentle · clamped, into the slot
    Edges
    overdrag resistance 3
    Scroll
    auto-scroll in a 72 px edge zone · ≤ 14 px/frame
    Keys
    Space lift · ↑↓ move · Space drop · Esc cancel

    Lifting is the only time hanji casts a shadow on a row: the item has left the page, so it reads as a physical thing in the hand.

      07.09

      Cascade

      The entrance for a list: each row fades and rises one step after the last. Past the cap every row shares the last delay, so a long list arrives as fast as a short one.

      Step
      24 ms per row
      Cap
      index 10 · later rows share 240 ms
      Rise
      12 × travel · gentle · clamped
      Fade
      base 260 · ease-out
      Once
      per surface · never on scroll-back
      Reduced
      no step, no rise · one 180 ms fade

      Choreography, not a crawl: at 24 ms a step uncapped, row 40 would land a second after row 1.

        07.10

        Morph stage

        A shared-element morph between two measured frames: the mini player becomes the player. One progress value drives everything, so a tap, a drag and an interruption are the same motion.

        Driver
        one value 0 → 1 · gentle · clamped
        Frames
        measured at open · art by transform
        Panel
        clip inset from the bar · radius 16 → 28
        Source
        fades out by .22
        Backdrop
        darkens until .55
        Content
        .35 → .88 · rises 28
        Drag
        projects .12 s of velocity · opens past .5
        Latch
        skip only if within .02 of target

        Interruptible by design: tap mid-flight and it turns around with its velocity intact. Under reduced motion the same value drives a cross-fade.
