Showcase

08

The opinionated, domain-flavoured pieces — audio and art — that prove the system can carry a real product. They live in @hanji/showcase, outside core: nothing in the component library knows what a track is, and these pieces are built only from what it exposes.

    08.01

    PlayingBars

    The “this one is playing” glyph: a few bars on periods that never fall into lockstep. It measures nothing and costs one value per bar. Rows that aren’t playing render their number, not a paused glyph.

    Bars
    2.5 wide · gap 2 · ×3
    Periods
    500 · 400 · 600 ms
    Ranges
    .25–1 · .40–.75 · .20–.90
    Pause
    settles to floor · 180 ms
    Reduced
    rest heights, never armed
    A11y
    hidden unless labelled

    Unrelated periods are what make three bars read as noise instead of one blinking shape. Height is scaleY from the foot — never an animated height.

    08.02

    DotSpectrum

    An ambient level display: columns of dots that ignite bottom-up from a signal the app supplies. Decoration — so it is the first thing reduced motion removes, and paused, hidden or switched off, it costs nothing at all.

    Grid
    28 × 7 · 3 px · gap 4
    Source
    12 Hz, throttled at source
    Ignition
    eighths · scale .6 → 1
    Presence
    α .22 · tapers to 40%
    Shaping
    1.65·raw.6 per delivery
    Idle
    one silent write, no timer

    Quantised output lets unchanged dots skip the write entirely — the counter below shows how few actually change on a tick (PRINCIPLES §17).

    Glass WeatherMira Okafor · Afterglow Studies
    In a dock — texture at α .22, behind the content, edge to edge
    The same field unmasked at α 1 — one source, one timer, two subscribers

    08.03

    Scrubber

    A seek bar over a fixed-point anchor {value, at, rate}. Motion is projected every frame, labels tick once a second, and nothing ever polls the player.

    Track
    4 · tint .24 · scaleX fill
    Thumb
    12 → ×1.6 while held
    Anchor
    on seek · pause · resume
    Labels
    1 Hz shared clock · mono
    Hold
    until within 2% · ≤ 2.5 s
    Keys
    ← → 5 s · PgUp/Dn 30 s

    A tap springs the thumb to the finger; a drag rides the same spring, so it can always be interrupted.

    08.04

    Generated art

    A painted stand-in for anything with no image: twelve palettes and eight compositions, chosen by hashing the id. A subject never changes its face. Rendered instead of an image, never under one.

    Hash
    FNV-1a → fmix32 · 3 salts
    Palettes
    12 · deep / mid / bright
    Shapes
    8 compositions
    Cost
    pure SVG · ≤ 25 nodes
    A11y
    hidden — row says the name
    API
    H.art(id, size) → svg

    Art is the one thing on a hanji screen allowed a shadow: one soft drop, so a cover reads as an object rather than a hole in the page.

    LiveSame id, same face — type to see

    Collage

    08.06

    Glyph cover

    A cover for things with no art by construction — a folder, a category, a smart list. A generated wash, a flat scrim, one glyph. Art alone says “we couldn’t read the image”; a glyph cover says “there is nothing to read”.

    Glyph
    16 · 20 · 24 · 28
    Steps
    at 48 · 72 · 120 px
    Scrim
    black · α .38 · flat
    Ink
    on-scrim, on every paper
    Icon
    app adapter · bold

    Stepped, never interpolated: a 56 px row cover and a 240 px hero are recognisably the same object, not one photo scaled.
