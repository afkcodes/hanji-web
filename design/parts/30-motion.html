Motion

02

hanji moves like a sheet of paper on a table: it answers the finger at once, travels a short way, and settles without fuss. Motion explains what changed — it is never the show. Every curve in this chapter is simulated from the library's own spring constants; switch Motion to Reduced above to watch the policy rewrite them.

    02.08

    The performance law

    Delight that drops frames is a bug with good taste. These are the rules behind every component above — each was a shipped bug on a real phone first, and a review rejection now.

    Steady state
    zero JavaScript per frame
    Lists
    60 fps over 1000 rows with art
    Gate
    release-build device pass before stable
    §1Animate transforms, never layout

    Width, height and top are skipped on gesture-driven frames in release builds: the thumb moves and the fill freezes. Draw full size inside a clip and move it.

    norae seek bar, dock line, slider, EQ · 2026-08-18/19
    §4Springs lie about being done

    Settled to the eye at 250 ms, alive to the maths at 500. Anything gated on “finished” uses a clamped spring or a timing.

    norae sheet back button · 2026-08-18
    §5One clock per surface

    Progress is an anchor — value, time, rate — projected per frame. A 1 Hz tick serves the labels. Never a second subscription.

    scrubber · zero-polling playhead
    §6Latches verify their skip

    An “already done” flag checks the world agrees before skipping, or an invisible overlay eats every touch.

    norae morph freeze · 2026-08-19
    §12Content mounts after the entrance

    A heavy subtree mounting during an entrance is a jerk. Let the surface land, then mount, holding the room with a quiet line.

    norae queue · 2026-08-18
    §17Quantise ambient motion

    Snap decorative outputs to coarse steps so unchanged frames cost nothing: 200 dots at 12 Hz contended with scrolling until snapped to eighths.

    norae dock spectrum · 2026-08-21
    §18Decoration releases its resources

    Paused, hidden or switched off costs zero — not even the sampler. The last subscriber turns the lights out.

    ambient layers
    §19Suspect the numbers before the machine

    A marquee “ran once and never came back” through four rewrites — its measured text had stretched to a 4000 px box. Assert the inputs first.
